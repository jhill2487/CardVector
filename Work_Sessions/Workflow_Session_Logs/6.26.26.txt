# Putnam Collectibles — Production Session Report

**Date:** June 26, 2026

## Objective

Validate the complete production workflow for creating and uploading a new batch of Pokémon listings using CardUploader and Putnam OS.

# Results

## Photography

Cards photographed: **100**

Elapsed time:

**17 minutes 30 seconds**

Average throughput:

Approximately **343 cards/hour**

Result:

Photography was faster than expected and is no longer believed to be the primary workflow bottleneck.

---

## Photo Transfer

Method:

iPhone connected via USB.

Time until photographs became available in Windows Explorer:

Approximately **15 minutes**

Observation:

This is now considered one of the largest workflow bottlenecks.

Future improvements should investigate:

* Camera format optimization
* Faster import workflow
* Scanner evaluation
* Automated import monitoring

# Production Bottlenecks Identified

## High Priority

1. iPhone → PC photo transfer.
2. Too many clicks required before CSV analysis begins.
3. Market Intelligence false positives.

## Medium Priority

1. Home page should prioritize the primary workflow.
2. Pricing Workspace should become workflow-driven instead of tool-driven.
